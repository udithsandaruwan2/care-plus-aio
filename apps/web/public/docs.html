Care Plus documentation

Public papers, architecture, and privacy notes for the NIBM research platform hosted at careplus.udithsandaruwan.com.

Care Plus is an AI-enhanced, voice-enabled personal healthcare assistant with integrated caregiver matching for chronic disease management in Sri Lanka. A large language model supports dialogue only; it does not decide caregiver rank.
